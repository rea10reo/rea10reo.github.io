Privacy Policy

Effective November 13, 2026

Boxer is a game made by RealOreo ("we"). It's designed to be fine for players of all ages, including kids. This policy explains what happens to information when you play.

The short version

  • We don't ask for your name, email, location or any account.
  • We don't collect or receive any personal information about you.
  • Your game progress stays on your own device.
  • Ads only play when you tap a Watch button, and they are never personalized.

Game progress

Your scores, dodges, unlocked gloves and settings are saved on your device. They are included in your device's iCloud or computer backup, like other app data. We never see them.

Purchases

Gloves you buy with real money are sold through Apple's App Store. Apple handles the payment. We only learn from Apple which gloves your Apple Account owns, so the game can unlock them. We never see your name, payment details or Apple Account.

Ads

Boxer offers optional "watch an ad" rewards using Google AdMob. An ad plays only when you tap a Watch button. Because kids play Boxer, every ad request is marked as directed to children and under the age of consent. This means Google limits what it collects and shows only non-personalized, general-audience (G-rated) ads. Boxer doesn't ask for permission to track you, and it doesn't use your device's advertising identifier.

To show an ad, Google's software may process limited technical information, such as your IP address, device type and operating system version, and how you interact with the ad. Google uses this to deliver the ad, count views and prevent fraud. Google handles this information under its own policies: Google Privacy Policy and how Google treats child-directed requests.

Children

We don't knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children under 13. There's no chat, no sign-up and no way to share personal details in the game. Parents can contact us with any question.

Deleting your data

Since we don't hold any data about you, there's nothing for us to delete. To erase your game progress, delete Boxer from your device.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update this page and the date above.
