Support

Boxer is a pixel boxing game for iPhone and iPad. You never throw a punch: swipe left or right to slip jabs, swipe down to lean back from uppercuts, and see how many punches you can survive.

Need help?

Send a question or report a problem here: contact Boxer support. Tell us which device you're playing on and what happened, and we'll get back to you.

Common questions

How do I dodge? Wait for the opponent's glove to flash white, then swipe: right to slip a jab from the left, left to slip a jab from the right, and down for an uppercut. Uppercuts join in after 20 dodges.

I bought gloves but they're gone. Open the Locker and tap Restore purchases. Use the same Apple Account you bought them with.

Will I lose my progress if I get a new phone? Your progress is saved on your device and comes along in your iCloud or computer backup when you move to a new iPhone or iPad.

Can I get a refund? Purchases are handled by Apple. You can ask for a refund at reportaproblem.apple.com.

Does Boxer have ads? Only if you choose to watch one. Some screens have a Watch button that plays a short ad to earn bonus dodges. Ads never play on their own.
